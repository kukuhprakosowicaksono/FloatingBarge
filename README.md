## 📌 Project Overview

The Floating Barge project involved the engineering development of a steel floating structure, covering hull structural detailing, deck outfitting, engineering drawings, material take-off, and fabrication support.

The project required coordination between engineering, production, and other project stakeholders to ensure that the design and drawings were suitable for fabrication and installation.

---

## 👨‍💻 My Role

**Naval Architect / Hull & Outfitting Engineer**

I was involved in the preparation, review, and development of engineering drawings and material requirements for the project, with a focus on hull structure and deck outfitting.

---

## 🛠️ Key Responsibilities

* Reviewed structural and outfitting drawings.
* Developed and revised hull structural drawings.
* Prepared deck outfitting arrangements and details.
* Prepared fabrication and installation drawings.
* Reviewed and prepared Material Take-Off (MTO).
* Developed structural details, supports, brackets, and connections.
* Coordinated engineering requirements with production.
* Revised drawings based on fabrication conditions.
* Supported engineering documentation for project requirements.
